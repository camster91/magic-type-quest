## Common Core State Standards (English Language Arts, Writing)

| Grade | Standard | What it asks for | BloomType levels that build it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | W.3.6 | Use technology to produce and publish writing, with guidance and support | Levels 1–4: home row, top row, bottom row, all letters |
| 4 | W.4.6 | Keyboarding well enough to type at least one page in a single sitting | Levels 4–7: all letters, capitals, numbers, speed |
| 5 | W.5.6 | Keyboarding well enough to type at least two pages in a single sitting | Levels 6–9: numbers, speed, accuracy, master words |
| 6 | W.6.6 | Keyboarding well enough to type at least three pages in a single sitting | Levels 8–10, plus Daily Moments for stamina |

## Ontario curriculum (Language, 2023)

The Ontario Language curriculum expects students to use digital tools to create texts. BloomType's French version supports French-language and French Immersion classrooms. Map this to specific expectations before an Ontario board pitch.

## Suggested pace

- **Grades 2–3:** Levels 1–3 over a term, 10 minutes a day.
- **Grades 4–5:** Levels 1–7 over a term, then Daily Moments.
- **Grades 6–8:** All 10 levels, with speed and accuracy goals set by the teacher.
