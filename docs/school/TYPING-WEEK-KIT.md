# Typing Week: a classroom event kit

A five-day class event that gets every student typing a little each day. It builds a habit and ends with a celebration. Students cooperate as a class; nobody is ranked against classmates.

## How class data works today

BloomType keeps progress **on each device**. Nothing goes to a server.

- **Shared class computers** (a lab or a cart that stays in your room): open the teacher page on each computer. It shows the students who played there.
- **One device per student, or devices that go home:** the teacher page on your computer can't see their progress. Run the week the same way, but count flowers by a show of hands and set assignments out loud.

Online class sign-in, which works across devices, stays off until the privacy reviews are done.

## Before the week

- Create a class on the teacher page and print the **student cards** (class code and sign-in steps).
- Print the **home-row poster** for the wall. On the teacher page, open your class and choose *📌 Home-row poster*.
- Set the class goal: **"Plant 100 flowers together."** Every student who practises on a day plants that day's flower. Track the class total on the poster.
- Optional: assign a level with a due date of Friday on the teacher page (shared computers only). On separate devices, write the level on the board.

## The five days (15 minutes a day)

| Day | Theme | In class | Teacher tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | **Roots:** the home row | Everyone starts Level 1. Show F and J bumps. | Walk around and check posture, not speed. |
| Tuesday | **Reach up:** the top row | Level 2, or continue Level 1. | Praise accuracy and stars, not words a minute. |
| Wednesday | **Dig down:** the bottom row | Level 3, or the class spelling words (paste them on the teacher page). | Spelling words double as typing practice. |
| Thursday | **Whole meadow** | Level 4, or a 60-second Daily Moment for everyone. | Students who struggle can turn on Relaxed mode. |
| Friday | **Bloom day** | Free choice, then the celebration. | Share the class total from the teacher report, or from a show of hands. |

## Celebrate

- Count the flowers the class planted this week and colour them in on the poster. Each student can see their own flowers in *My Garden*.
- Print a **certificate** for every student (*🏅 Certificate* on the teacher page). Write their name by hand.
- Share one class number with families, such as "Our class practised 312 minutes this week!", not individual scores.

## After the week

- Keep a 10-minute slot two or three times a week.
- Make sure every student has a **save code** so they can recover progress on a new device. Students copy theirs from *Profile*. On shared class computers you can also use *🔑 Print save codes* on the teacher page.
- On shared computers, watch for the warning at the top of the teacher report: it lists how many students haven't played in 7+ days.
