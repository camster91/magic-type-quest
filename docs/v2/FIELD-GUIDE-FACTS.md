# Field Guide facts and sources

Each Field Guide card shows one short fact a young reader can understand. Every fact must have a source before it ships. Translations must say the same thing as the English.

| Card | Fact (English) | Source to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Monarch butterfly | Monarch caterpillars eat only milkweed leaves. | U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, monarch species page; National Wildlife Federation, Monarch Butterfly guide |
| Bumble bee | Bumble bees carry pollen in little baskets on their back legs. | Xerces Society, bumble bee pages (pollen baskets / corbiculae) |
| Eastern bluebird | Bluebirds nest in tree holes and in nest boxes people put up. | Cornell Lab of Ornithology, All About Birds: Eastern Bluebird |
| Milkweed | Milkweed seeds float away on soft, silky fluff. | USDA Forest Service, Plant of the Week: common milkweed |

**Status:** these are widely documented facts, but the owner or a teacher reviewer should open each source and tick it off before a school release.

## Rules for new cards

- One sentence, at a grade 2–3 reading level.
- True for the species in the picture, not just its relatives.
- Kind and calm: no predators eating, no danger or extinction alarm.
- Add the fact here with its source in the same pull request.
