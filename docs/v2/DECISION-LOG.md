# Nature Quest v2 decision log

## 2026-09-24 — grounded wildlife restoration

**Decision:** The v2 child experience uses wildlife, habitats, field research, and visible restoration caused by demonstrated typing mastery. Replace BloomType's garden-first and magic-adjacent framing in v2. Do not carry over its supernatural vocabulary, punitive ordinary-error lives, or decorative reward economy. Preserve recoverable v1 code, data, and assets while v2 is built in isolation.

**Reason:** The product direction and explicit constraints in [#153](https://github.com/camster91/magic-type-quest/issues/153) and [#154](https://github.com/camster91/magic-type-quest/issues/154) require a grounded nature game whose learning progress changes the world. See [PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md](PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md) for the acceptance test for future content and features.

**Scope:** This record changes the direction for new v2 content. It does not rename the repository, migrate learner data, alter the live v1 route, or authorize later biome expansion.
