# BloomType homeschool typing guide

A simple plan for teaching touch typing at home with BloomType. It is free for families, works offline, needs no account, and keeps your child's progress on your own device.

## How it works

- **10 levels**, each a part of the keyboard: home row, top row, bottom row, all letters, capitals, numbers, then speed, accuracy and mastery.
- **One short session a day beats one long one.** 10–15 minutes is plenty.
- **Accuracy first, speed later.** Stars are earned for accuracy, never for speed.
- **No losing.** Missing a day costs nothing. Each day your child practises, they plant that day's flower in their meadow.

## Pick a track

| Track | Ages | Daily time | Levels | About how long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sprout | 7–8 | 10 minutes | 1–4 | One term (12 weeks) |
| Bloom | 9–10 | 10–15 minutes | 1–7 | One term (12 weeks) |
| Meadow | 11–13 | 15 minutes | 1–10 | One term, then Daily Moments to keep it up |

Move on when your child is ready, not by the calendar. "Ready" means 3 stars (95%+ accuracy) on the level, two days in a row.

## A 12-week plan (Bloom track)

| Weeks | Focus | Level | Goal by the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Home row, posture, eyes on the screen | 1 | 3 stars without looking at the keys |
| 3–4 | Top row | 2 | 3 stars |
| 5–6 | Bottom row | 3 | 3 stars |
| 7–8 | All letters together | 4 | 3 stars, about 10 words a minute |
| 9 | Capitals with the opposite hand's Shift | 5 | 2–3 stars |
| 10 | Numbers | 6 | 2–3 stars |
| 11–12 | Speed, with accuracy kept high | 7 | About 15–20 words a minute at 90%+ |

**Sprout track:** spend 3 weeks on each of levels 1–4.
**Meadow track:** follow the Bloom plan, then add levels 8–10 at one per week, with Daily Moments on the other days.

## The daily routine (10–15 minutes)

1. **Warm up (1 minute).** Fingers on the home row. Find the bumps on F and J.
2. **Play the current level (5–10 minutes).** If your child is frustrated, turn on *Relaxed mode* in Profile → Reading and play, so the words wait for them.
3. **Practise weak keys (2–3 minutes).** Use the 🎯 Practice screen, or *Drill Weak Keys* when it appears after a round. It picks the keys your child misses most.
4. **Finish on a win.** Stop after a good round, not a hard one.

## Tracking progress

- **Parents page** (`parents.html`): weekly minutes, days practised, keys learned and best speed for each child on the device.
- **Homeschool record:** on the Parents page, choose *Print homeschool record* for a dated log of practice and levels finished. Many states and provinces accept this as a work sample.
- **Save code:** under Profile → My Save Code. Write it down so progress can move to another device.

## Tips

- **Posture:** feet flat, wrists floating, screen at eye level.
- **Don't look down.** A light cloth over the hands helps after level 1.
- **Siblings:** each child gets their own player under Profile → Players on this device.
- **Grown-up lock:** set a PIN under Profile so only an adult can delete progress or change settings.
- **Easier reading:** Profile → Reading and play has an easy-read font, OpenDyslexic and high contrast.

## Questions families ask

**Is it really free?** Yes. Family use is free with no ads. Schools pay for teacher tools.

**Does it collect my child's data?** No. Everything stays in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**What speed should my child reach?** Accuracy matters more. As a rough guide: ages 8–9 about 10–15 words a minute, ages 10–11 about 15–25, ages 12+ about 25–35, all at 90%+ accuracy.
