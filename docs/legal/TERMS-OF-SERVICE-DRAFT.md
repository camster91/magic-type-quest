## 8. Your data stays on your device

In the public version of BloomType, **progress stays on the device**. It is saved in the browser's local storage on the computer, tablet or Chromebook the child uses. It is not sent to us.

This means:

- **You are in control.** You can download or delete a child's progress at any time from the Profile screen. Teachers can export a class report and clear all BloomType data on their device. Clearing the browser's site data also deletes everything.
- **We cannot recover it.** We do not have a copy. If the browser's storage is cleared (some school Chromebooks do this between users), progress is lost unless you kept a **save code** or a downloaded file.
- **You look after the device.** Anyone who can use the device can see the progress saved on it. Use the teacher PIN and grown-up lock where they help.

Our [privacy policy](PRIVACY-POLICY-DRAFT.md) explains exactly what is saved and how to delete it.
