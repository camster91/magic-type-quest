# Outreach templates

Short messages for homeschool influencers, co-op leaders and teacher Facebook groups, plus a follow-up and a tracking table.

> **Draft for the owner.** Keep it personal and honest:
> - Write to one person at a time. Use their name and mention something real they made.
> - Lead with **free for families**. Never offer payment for a good review, and never ask for one.
> - School prices are **proposed**, and cloud class login is **not live yet**. Don't promise either.
> - Don't send more than one follow-up. If they say no, thank them and stop.
> - Never ask for children's names, photos or data.

Fill in everything in `[square brackets]`. Links:

- Landing page: https://bloomtype.ashbi.ca/magic-type-quest/landing.html
- Play now: https://bloomtype.ashbi.ca/magic-type-quest/
- Homeschool guide: [CURRICULUM-GUIDE.md](../homeschool/CURRICULUM-GUIDE.md) (share it as a PDF or web page)
- Typing Week kit: [TYPING-WEEK-KIT.md](../school/TYPING-WEEK-KIT.md)

## 3. Teacher Facebook group post

Read the group's rules first. Many groups ban self-promotion or allow it only on certain days. Ask an admin if you're not sure.

> **Free typing game for grades 1–7: looking for honest feedback**
>
> Hi all. I'm [Your name], and I make BloomType, a touch-typing game for kids 6 to 12. I'm sharing it because it's free for students and families, with no ads.
>
> - No student accounts and no emails. Progress is saved on the device.
> - A free teacher page for one class on one computer: class codes, assignments, spelling-word lessons and CSV export.
> - Works offline. English, French and Spanish.
>
> Honest limits: progress is saved per device right now, so it works best on shared class computers or with each student's save code. Online class sign-in across devices isn't live yet.
>
> Try it here: [landing page link]. I'd really value feedback from anyone who teaches keyboarding.

**Rules for the table:**
- Set the follow-up date to 7–10 days after sending. Leave it blank after one follow-up.
- Write "no" and stop if they ask you to stop. Delete their details if they ask.
- Review it once a week. Keep a count of tries, replies and shares. That's enough to see what works.
